# Code of conduct

Be kind, be specific, assume good faith.

- Critique the work, never the person.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Respect people's time: clear issues, small pull requests, honest descriptions.
- Never share someone else's private data, including page content from their ledger.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through GitHub.
